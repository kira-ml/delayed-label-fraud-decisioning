## Notes for the Paper Team

- Do not cite a reference that is not used in the paper.
- Do not use a claim from a source without a citation.
- Format every entry as: authors, "title," in *venue*, year, pages.
- Journal articles use *Journal Name*, vol. X, no. Y, pp. Z, Year.
- Conference papers use in *Proc. Name*, Year, pp. Z.
- IEEE style uses numbered brackets [1], [2], … in text, and this list
  preserves that order.
